# LunaRangers Unity

Open this `unity` folder in Unity Hub with Unity **6000.2.7f2**. In the Editor, choose **LunaRangers → Open Day 1**, then press Play. Day 1 is also the enabled scene in the build scene list.

Move with WASD or the arrow keys. Press E or click the prompt to interact. At the heater, drag the three plugs to their matching sockets, click each plug and socket, or use 1, 2, and 3. Use the controls on each equipment panel to solve its puzzle. Select **Show hint** if you get stuck, Esc to close a panel, or R to restart the day.

The crew habitat connects to the west workshop and east lab through the marked passages. Three larger missions involve retrieving the battery and wiring the heater, rebuilding the recycling pipes, and scanning and excavating a Moon rock. The four shorter challenges cover power allocation, filter selection, shelter shielding, and sample identification. Suit timers run outdoors only. After completing Day 7, select **Replay mission** to return to Day 1.

The standalone campaign runs on a local training simulation. Suit and solar alert times and resource changes are gameplay values. The outpost scene and its solar and airlock bridge scripts are available under `Assets/TechnicalResearch` for browser integration with the team's React frontend and simulation.
