# LunaRangers Unity

Open this `unity` folder in Unity Hub with Unity **6000.2.7f2**. In the Editor, choose **LunaRangers → Open Day 1**, then press Play. Day 1 is also the enabled scene in the build scene list.

Move with WASD or the arrow keys. Press E or click the prompt to interact. Retrieve the spare battery, return through the airlock before the ten-second suit timer expires, dock the battery, and connect both heater wires. Choose plugs and sockets with the on-screen buttons or keys 1 and 2. Press R to restart.

Day 1 runs on a local training simulation. Its timer and resource changes are gameplay values. The outpost scene and its solar and airlock bridge scripts are available under `Assets/TechnicalResearch` for browser integration. The team simulation and React frontend are separate integration work.
